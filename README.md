
# My Favorite Series

## One Piece

**One Piece** is my favorite anime series. It follows the adventures of Monkey D. Luffy and his crew as they travel across the sea in search of the legendary treasure called One Piece.

### Main Characters
- Monkey D. Luffy
- Roronoa Zoro
- Nami
- Sanji
- Usopp

### Why I Like This Series
1. It has an interesting story.
2. The characters have unique personalities.
3. It teaches friendship, courage, and never giving up.

### Favorite Quote
> A person's dream never ends.

### Rating
**10/10**
